## Notes

- Separate criteria apply to **Vietnam Ho Chi Minh City Transport University 2+2 Transfer Students (Spring 2024 – Fall 2026)**.
- International student scholarships (**A–E**) will be awarded to students who submit a language proficiency certificate **before the start of each semester**, according to the corresponding level.
- If a student later acquires a **higher-level certificate** during enrollment, the **maximum scholarship amount** they can receive is **International Student Scholarship C**.
- Proof of English proficiency applies only to students in the **English track**, and there is **no expiration date** for submitted certificates.
