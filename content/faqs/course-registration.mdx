## Information on How to Register for Courses (Undergraduate)

### Step-by-step guide

1. **Log in** to the Tongmyong University course registration site
– Enter the site address directly in your browser.

2. **Login credentials**
– **ID:** Student ID
– **Password:** Same password used for `my.tu.ac.kr`

3. Click **“Sign up for a course.”**

4. Select your desired courses based on:
- Department graduation requirements
- Recommended year of study

> **Note:**
> From the **third year onward**, it is strongly recommended to consult your **academic advisor during Advising Week** and register only for courses that meet graduation requirements.

---

### Credits applied for

- **Undergraduate:**
Apply according to graduation credit requirements
_(Scholarship applies when registering for **at least 12 credits**)_

- **Special cases:**
When registering for a course, please contact the **teaching assistant** in your department office before applying.

- **Graduate limits:**
- **Master’s degree:** up to **6 credits**
- **Ph.D.:** up to **9 credits**

> Check **“Course registration information”** in the notices on the homepage every semester.
